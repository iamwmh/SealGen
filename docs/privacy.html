隐私声明

生效日期：2026年10月11日。本声明适用于 macOS 应用 SealGen（印章）。

开发者

SealGen 由 Minghao Wang 开发。应用不要求注册账号，也没有开发者运营的服务器。

我们不收集的内容

SealGen 不收集、不上传、不出售个人信息。这包括姓名、电子邮件、位置、联系人、照片图库、广告标识，以及你在印面上输入的文字。

应用没有分析、广告或跨应用跟踪。你的印稿、图片和文字不会发送给开发者，也不会发送到开发者的服务器。

留在这台 Mac 上的内容

设计在本机完成。你添加的文字、纹样，以及通过系统文件选择器导入的图片，只用于在印面上排版。导出 STL、3MF、OBJ 或 PNG 时，文件写入你在系统保存对话框中选择的位置。

应用不会自行把这些文件同步到云端。如果你把导出的文件放进 iCloud 云盘或其他由你控制的位置，那是 macOS 或你自己的选择，不是 SealGen 的功能。

系统诊断

Apple 可能按照你的 macOS 设置收集崩溃或诊断信息。SealGen 没有加入自己的统计或崩溃上报。

儿童

SealGen 不以 13 岁以下儿童为对象，也不会向任何人收集个人信息。

声明的变更

如果这项声明有实质更新，会改写本页并更换上面的生效日期。

联系

隐私问题可以通过 App Store 上 SealGen 的开发者页面联系 Minghao Wang。

Privacy policy

Effective October 11, 2026. This policy covers the SealGen app for macOS.

Developer

SealGen is made by Minghao Wang. The app does not ask you to create an account, and the developer does not operate a server for it.

Data the app does not collect

SealGen does not collect, upload, or sell personal information. That includes your name, email address, location, contacts, photo library, advertising identifier, and the characters you type on a seal.

The app has no analytics, advertising, or tracking across apps. Your designs, images, and text are not sent to the developer or to a server run by the developer.

What stays on your Mac

Designing happens on the device. Characters, motifs, and images you import through the system file picker are used only to lay out the seal face. When you export STL, 3MF, OBJ, or PNG, the file is written to the place you choose in the system save dialog.

The app does not sync those files to a cloud service by itself. If you save an export in iCloud Drive or another location you control, that is macOS or your own choice, not a SealGen feature.

System diagnostics

Apple may collect crash or diagnostic information under your macOS settings. SealGen does not add its own analytics or crash reporting.

Children

SealGen is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to Minghao Wang through the developer page for SealGen on the App Store.
